## 📌 Project Overview
The E-Commerce Dashboard provides an interactive overview of sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product popularity, customer ratings, and shipping efficiency.
The dashboard helps stakeholders understand how customers shop, which products are popular across customer segments, how sales change over time, and where improvements can be made to enhance customer satisfaction and business performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- 	Microsoft Excel — Dashboard development and data visualization
- 	PowerQuery — Used to transform data. (Add columns like Days to deliver, Week number, and Gender)
- 	PivotTables and Charts — Data summarization and analysis
- 	Slicers — Interactive filtering and exploration
- 	Data Visualization — Charts, conditional formatting, and geographic maps

## 🎯 Project Objectives
-	Monitor key e-commerce performance indicators.
-	Analyze sales and order quantity trends for the first 13 weeks of 2025 (January to March).
-	Understand customer preferences across different order channels and genders.
-	Identify popular products and purchasing patterns.
-	Analyze geographic sales and order distribution across California counties.
-	Evaluate shipping and delivery performance.
-	Explore customer satisfaction through product and order ratings.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard displays the following headline metrics:
- Total Orders	
- Total Quantity	
- Total Sales Amount	
- Average Rating	
- Average Days to Deliver
The values will change based on the slicers.

## 📈 Dashboard Features
**1. Sales and Quantity Trends**
  -	Tracks total quantity and sales amount for the first 13 weeks of 2025 (January to March.
  -	Highlights weekly changes in sales performance and purchasing volume.
    
**2. Customer Purchasing Preferences**
  -	Compares order channels across Female, Male, Other, and Unknown customer segments.
  -	Includes App, Instagram, Partner App, Target.com, and Website.
  -	Uses a color-coded matrix to compare the distribution of customer segments across channels.
    
**3. Purchase Quantity Analysis**
  -	Examines how many items customers purchase per order.
  -	Groups orders by quantities such as 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6–10, and more than 10 items.
    
**4. Popular Products by Gender**
  -	Compares product popularity across customer segments.
  -	Highlights products such as T-Shirts, Jeans, Sneakers, Tank Tops, Bikinis, Shorts, and more.
  -	Combines a stacked bar chart with a doughnut chart for the Gender segment comparisons.
    
**5. Geographic Sales Analysis**
  -	Visualizes sales amounts and order distribution across California counties.
  -	Helps identify geographic areas that may warrant further business analysis.

**6. Shipping and Delivery Analysis**
  -	Displays the distribution of orders by shipping duration.
  -	Helps identify common delivery times and orders with longer shipping periods.
    
**7. Customer Rating Analysis**
  -	Compares monthly order counts by customer rating.
  -	Shows rating distributions for January, February, and March.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Filters
The dashboard includes slicers for:
-	Gender: Female, Male, Other, Unknown
-	Order Mode: App, Instagram, Partner App, Target.com, Website
  
These filters allow users to explore customer behavior and compare performance across different segments and order channels.

## 🔍 Business Questions
This dashboard helps answer the following questions:
1.	How do sales amounts and quantities change for the first 13 weeks of 2025 (January to March)?
2.	Which purchasing channels are most popular among different customer segments?
3.	How many items do customers typically purchase per order?
4.	Which products are most popular across gender categories?
5.	Which California counties contribute the most sales and orders?
6.	How long does shipping typically take, and how frequently are deliveries delayed?
7.	How do customer ratings vary from January through March?
8.	Which customer segments or product categories may offer growth opportunities?

## 🚀 Business Recommendations
-	*Improve customer experience*: Investigate ratings below the desired satisfaction level and identify recurring customer concerns.
-	*Optimize delivery operations*: Monitor longer shipping durations and investigate potential delivery bottlenecks.
-	*Strengthen product strategy*: Use product popularity analysis to guide inventory planning and promotional campaigns.
-	*Personalize marketing*: Compare customer segments and order channels to develop targeted marketing strategies.
-	*Focus on geographic opportunities*: Investigate high-performing counties and evaluate opportunities in lower-performing areas.
-	*Monitor sales trends*: Review weekly sales and quantity movements to support inventory and promotional planning.

## 📌 Conclusion
This project demonstrates how interactive dashboards can turn e-commerce data into actionable business information. By combining sales trends, product analysis, customer segmentation, geographic visualization, shipping performance, and ratings, the dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance.
It highlights the value of data visualization in monitoring KPIs, identifying opportunities for improvement, and supporting data-driven business decisions.
